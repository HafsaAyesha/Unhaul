Before you start

Unhaul is designed to observe product pages on Daraz Pakistan to help you reconsider purchases. Product observation is not enabled in this version; the current content script only checks its connection to the extension.

Your items, product visits, and saved list stay in this browser.

When AI classification is enabled, the public product title and image are sent through our classification service to an AI provider. Your owned items, visit history, and saved list are not included.

AI classification is not enabled in this version. No product information is currently sent for AI classification.

Unhaul does not read payment details or block checkout.

Before you start

With your agreement, Unhaul observes product pages and Add-to-cart interactions on these supported stores only: Daraz, Stationers.pk, The Stationery Company, Zubaida's, Rung, The School Store, Saleemi Book Depot, Toy Company, Toynix, Odeez, Baby Bazar, Chase Value, Design Your Own, Blingspot, SOZ, Nishat Linen, Bonanza Satrangi, Cross Stitch, Junaid Jamshed, Outfitters and Zellbury, to help you reconsider purchases. An interaction is not proof of a purchase.

Your items, product visits, and saved list stay in this browser.

When AI classification is enabled, the public product title, category text and image are sent through our classification service to an AI provider. Your owned items, visit history, and saved list are not included.

AI classification is not configured in this build. No product information is currently sent for AI classification.

Unhaul does not read payment details or block checkout.

While an item you saved is cooling off, Unhaul holds Add to cart for that item on supported stores. You can change this in Settings.

You can also turn Unhaul on for other stores one at a time; Chrome will ask you each time.

Use Unhaul on this site?

Unhaul will read product pages on this site to compare them with your items. Your data stays in this browser.
